# Modern Financial Tracker Features (2024-2025)

To transition from a simple manual tracker to a "great" financial assistant, consider these features:

## 1. AI-Driven Automation
- **Predictive Analytics:** Use machine learning to forecast future cash flow based on past behavior.
- **Spending Warnings:** Proactively warn users if they are on track to overspend before it happens.
- **Smart Categorization:** Automatically categorize transactions using NLP or LLMs.

## 2. Subscription Management
- **Identify Recurring Charges:** Automatically flag subscriptions and recurring bills.
- **Negotiation/Cancellation:** Integration with services that can negotiate lower bills or simplify cancellation.

## 3. Conversational AI Assistants
- **Natural Language Queries:** "Can I afford a $50 dinner tonight?" or "How much did I spend on coffee this month?"
- **Contextual Insights:** "You've spent 20% more on dining out this month compared to last."

## 4. Collaborative Budgeting
- **Shared Views:** Allow couples/partners to manage combined finances with separate logins.
- **Goal Tracking:** Joint savings goals with progress tracking.

## 5. Holistic Wealth Management
- **Net Worth Tracking:** Include investments, property, and liabilities.
- **Investment Monitoring:** Real-time tracking of stocks, crypto, and other assets.
- **Retirement Planning:** Tools to project future wealth and retirement readiness.

## 6. Connectivity & Security
- **Reliable Bank Syncing:** Integration with aggregators like Plaid, Salt Edge, or local equivalents (e.g., Brankas in SE Asia).
- **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** Essential for protecting sensitive financial data.
- **Bank-Level Encryption:** AES-256 for data at rest and TLS for data in transit.

## 7. User Experience (UX)
- **Zero-Based Budgeting:** Every dollar gets a job (YNAB style).
- **"In My Pocket" Feature:** Simple display of spendable cash after bills and goals (PocketGuard style).
- **Customizable Dashboards:** Allow users to prioritize the metrics they care about most.
